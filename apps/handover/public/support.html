Common questions

  • Camera won't scan: allow camera access in Settings, or paste the handover link on the Take over screen.
  • Data and privacy: everything stays on your device unless you hand over jobs. See the privacy policy.
  • Safe use: use clinical shorthand only. Do not enter patient-identifiable information.

App information

Handover is a personal project by Dr Khalid Shamiyah. It is a ward task list, not a patient record or medical device. It does not provide medical advice.
